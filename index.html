The problem

Congestion is a behaviour problem — not only an infrastructure one.

Cities have spent decades adding lanes, signals and sensors. But traffic is the sum of thousands of individual decisions — when to leave, which route to take, whether to drive at all. The moment conditions change — a new toll, a closed bridge, a concert, a wet Monday — people respond, and the whole network shifts. Infrastructure tells you what a road can carry. It can't tell you how people will choose to use it.

Roads don't cause congestion — decisions do.

Every jam is thousands of people choosing the same road at the same time.

Static models assume behaviour stays fixed.

It doesn't. People adapt to price, time pressure and disruption — continuously.

Sensors capture the present, not the response.

They show what's happening now — not what happens to a change you're considering.

The mechanism behind the outcomes

Human Behaviour Prediction

Trafiqo doesn't just watch where cars are. It models how thousands of people decide at once — when price, congestion or time pressure changes.

A behaviour engine for urban mobility: thousands of synthetic agents that respond differently to pricing, time pressure, events and routing — so you can test a change before it ever reaches the street.

If you work in transport planning, this is behavioural / travel-demand modelling — rebuilt to run continuously, at city scale, on live conditions.

Modelled, not monitored

Synthetic agents are simulated populations, not real people. Trafiqo models aggregated, anonymised behaviour — never named individuals.

How it works

How Trafiqo works

1

Inputs

Live traffic, road-network and demand data, pricing signals, policies, weather and events — approved and provided through city partners.

2

Behavioural simulation engine

Trafiqo runs a simulated population of synthetic agents that respond to those conditions the way real travellers would: choosing routes, times and modes — or staying put.

3

Outputs

Optimised traffic distribution, pricing recommendations, and congestion / travel-time / emissions insight — delivered as decision-support your teams review and act on.

No black-box decisions. Every output is documented, testable and human-reviewed.
